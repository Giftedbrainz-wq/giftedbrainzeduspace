Gifted Brainz v11.3.4

CBT question-image reliability update:
- Question images are now fetched through the authenticated client as Blob URLs before being rendered in the CBT.
- This avoids direct <img> loading races with the short-lived file-access cookie.
- Large question images use bounded 4 MB range requests, matching the existing material-delivery safeguards.
- The behavior applies to standard CBTs, Question Bank practice, and multi-subject question sets because they share the same exam renderer.
- Existing question-image upload, CBT scoring, progressive practice, subject scoping, and material download behavior is preserved.
