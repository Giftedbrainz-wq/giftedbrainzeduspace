Gifted Brains EduSpace v11.3.18 — Student-Friendly Explanation Update

- Imported explanations are now cleaned and checked for extraction/meta wording.
- Explanations containing source/extraction commentary are replaced with a clear student-facing explanation generated from the question, options and correct answer.
- Number-base conversions receive step-by-step explanations showing place values/powers and the resulting numeral.
- Binary-to-decimal conversions receive step-by-step power-of-two explanations.
- Common percentage calculations receive a direct worked explanation.
- Existing question-bank records are upgraded during normalisation so previously imported weak explanations can be replaced.
- Raw <sub>/<sup> tags are handled as semantic math markup instead of being exposed as ordinary text.
- Caret notation is converted to semantic superscript markup.
- Existing platform architecture and APIs are preserved.
