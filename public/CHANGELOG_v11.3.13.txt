v11.3.13 — student identity, WhatsApp and extractor hardening
- Extractor preserves DOCX superscript/subscript formatting as semantic <sup>/<sub> markup and converts common caret/underscore math notation safely.
- Extracted question/option/explanation text is compacted to remove unnecessary gaps while preserving question structure.
- Admin recent results now show the submitting student's name and username.
- Student registration now requires a WhatsApp number; admins can open a direct WhatsApp chat from pending/student records.
- Self-deleted accounts now release their username/email uniqueness reservations so those credentials can be registered again.
- Registration also cleans up stale uniqueness reservations left by previously deleted accounts.
- Passwords remain non-unique by design, so a password previously used by a deleted account can be used again.
